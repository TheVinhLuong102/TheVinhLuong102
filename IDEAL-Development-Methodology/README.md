# `IDEAL` Development Methodology

`IDEAL` is a development methodology for effectively building, governing and growing
a technological initiative/project by writing and maintaining a collection
of documents spanning multiple abstraction levels:

- Level 0: [Implementation](0-Implementation)
- Level 1: [Design](1-Design)
- Level 2: [Ecosystem & Environment](2-Ecosystem-and-Environment)
- Level 3: [Architecture](3-Architecture)
- Level 4: [Law & Principle](4-Law-and-Principle)

Technological development is an iterative process regularly switching back-and-forth
between the pattern-recognizing specific-to-general `IDEAL` order
and the reverse pattern-applying general-to-specific `LAEDI` ("lady") order.

The below sections overview the roles of each of these types of document
in the high-to-low, general-to-specific `LAEDI` order.

## Level 4: [Law & Principle](4-Law-and-Principle)

Level-4 Law-and-Principle documents lay out
the key fundamental beliefs, philosophies and viewpoints
underpinning the concerned technological initiative/project.

## Level 3: [Architecture](3-Architecture)

Given the above Level-4 Law-and-Principle beliefs, philosophies and viewpoints,
Level-3 Architecture documents provide or suggest
abstract fundamental building blocks or major components
that constitute technological systems based on or faithfully following
such beliefs, philosophies and viewpoints.

## Level 2: [Ecosystem & Environment](2-Ecosystem-and-Environment)

Given the above Level-3 Architecture fundamental building blocks or major components,
Level-2 Ecosystem-and-Environment documents enumerate the up-to-date-today's-landscape
major technological ecosystem players and environmental forces
that conveniently/readily provide and/or strongly inform/dictate
the ensuing technological design choices
that the concerned technological initiative/project under development
should consider and opt to align/co-exist harmoniously with,
for the sake of the initiative/project's adoption, growth and success.

## Level 1: [Design](1-Design)

Given the above Level-2 Ecosystem-and-Environment awareness
and informed inclinations/orientations for key technological choices
Level-1 Design documents draw up granular, ready-for-implementation designs
for the technological initiative/project.

Level-1 Design documents may include multiple design options
and/or iterative phasing and/or conditional trial-and-error approaches.

## Level 0: [Implementation](0-Implementation)

Given the above Level-1 Design documents,
Level-0 Implementation documents detail out implementation phases and steps
that can be iteratively tracked and/or updated along the implementation progression.
